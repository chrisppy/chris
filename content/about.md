I'm a software engineer with 14+ years of experience building enterprise systems
for federal programs including USPS, FAA, and DHS.

I work primarily with Go, Java, and Rust, with experience across backend
services, desktop applications, web services, data integration, and
containerized systems.

I'm also interested in the tooling and infrastructure that surrounds software
development — CI/CD, reproducible builds, Linux, and declarative configuration.

These days, a lot of my personal work revolves around NixOS, self-hosting,
open-source software, and Rust.

I enjoy building things that are reliable, reproducible, and pleasantly boring
to operate.
